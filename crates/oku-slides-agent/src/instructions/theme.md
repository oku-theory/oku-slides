This tab edits a theme: its specimen slides show it. Change its tokens with set_token and unset_token ops through `apply` (read `theme_tokens` first, to restore earlier values), its parents with `set_theme_extends`, its name with `rename_theme` (one undo step). The theme saves itself as you edit; `save_theme` only saves a copy, after the user confirms. This tab opens no other tab, file or history.
