# oku-slides-session

Editing session of one deck, its workspace (windows, tabs, resume), the PowerPoint import; the app and `serve` expose it.

## Session

- `Session` (`lib.rs`; snapshot, SVG and slide detail in `session_view.rs`) answers `Request` through `Session::handle` (`request.rs`, classifiers in `request_class.rs`; errors in `error.rs`, their stable kinds in `error_kind.rs`): one deck (`SourceBundle`, Loro replica, undo by
  the non-persisted `UndoManager`), its rendering, pack mode, resume (`resume.rs`, `resume_writer.rs`, `resume_pack.rs`, `resume_theme.rs`, `resume_versions.rs`, `resume_workshop.rs`: old workshops reopen on their deck; `resume_read.rs`: one read budget per tab, each entry charged), file (`deck_file.rs`, `files.rs`, `merge.rs`; `sources_budget.rs`: assets, packs, themes, an import or merge that would pass `[sources] max_sources_mib` are refused before any change), agent usage (`deck_usage.rs`: never dirty, but unsaved for a close while history is open; re-embedded without render when a clean tab closes (a close refuses one no close tried), routed by `workspace_usage.rs`, queued while the deck sleeps; `[usage] store_in_deck` off records none and leaves files as they are).
- `Mode`: `Deck`, `Help` (`help.rs`), `Theme` (`theme_tab.rs`: a library theme's draft on a read-only specimen deck, saved and leased by `theme_tab_save.rs`; `document_kind.rs` refuses what a kind does not take, caller knowledge ops outside a deck tab), `Pack`, `History`; the last two set the deck aside (`preview` for a pack tab, a copy of its opener's deck or help, `pack_tab.rs`; `stash` for history; `pack_lease.rs` leases a tab's pack or theme; a deck-only pack's tab (`deck_pack_tab.rs`) is linked to its owner (`deck_pack_link.rs`, `deck_pack_receive.rs`), forwarded by `workspace_deck_packs*.rs`): `history.rs`,
  `pack_mode*.rs` (a slide `layout-<name>` per layout, preview of its example; read-only `widget-<name>` per widget), `layout_examples.rs`, `pack_library.rs`, `preview_theme.rs` (a pack tab's silent preview theme); `present.rs` (`can_present`, one refusal per mode, `Snapshot.can_present`).
- Packs (`deck.widgets`, `deck.layouts`; a namespace in one kind only) and themes: embedded versions are authoritative, a deck-only
  pack never reaches the library; received versions enter it only once the user validates them: `received_review.rs` lists them (`Snapshot.received_to_review`, with `Capabilities` cut at 50 names and `totals`, relation and blocker, cached on pins, library writes and approvals), `received_held.rs` walks the pins and says what the library lacks (`library_has`, also the deck-mode widget write gate), `pack_receive.rs` installs (`AcceptReceived`, the panel's `add_to_library`; a pack tab on one not installed: `PackNotInstalled`). Panel `host-packs`: `deck_packs.rs` (text-free
  `DeckPacksView`, add/remove/use mine/make current/theme update, cached `pack_updates`; `deck_offer.rs` (opener offers: a version, a switch to a saved copy, a theme version); `deck_only_offer.rs` (removed deck-only packs, added back from the sources); `library_index.rs` counts library writes (`WRITES`)), `deck_packs_panel.rs` (surface, events; `packs_event_for`: a button's event for the agent), `deck_packs_guide.rs` (each pinned pack's and the theme's use guide, read from the embedded versions as the agent reads them, folded and read-only, with its origin; awaiting approval, its edit guide too and an approval button; `ApproveGuides` is a user click from the interface, rechecked against the pins). Built-ins `base` (widgets) and `essentiel` (layouts), sources from render, pinned by producers
  (new deck with no pin: `adopt_default_packs`; help; imports, built-in version; pack mode), never on open; current built-ins
  resolve in-process (`deck_widgets.rs`, `probed_widgets`: `builtin_registry` for exactly both), no packs `WidgetRegistry::default()`.
  `deck_fallback.rs` never disables a shipped built-in; `deck_closure.rs` (`requires`, dependents, `examples_requires`); `deck_pack.rs`,
  `deck_theme.rs` embed defaults; `library_packs.rs` (`PackLibrary`: a `Library<K>` per kind of `library_kind.rs`, a `ThemeLibrary`
  clone; namespace unique across packs and themes, `deck`/`deck-layouts` reserved, built-ins never owned by a user copy (`owned_current`); `namespace_slug.rs`, `library.rs` (`library_draft.rs`: drafts replaced in place until a deck adopts them, `adopted`), `library_index.rs`, `library_orphans.rs` (resume deletes a `created-empty` draft whose tab never opened, a day old); `deck_adopt.rs` freezes a deck's pins).
- Themes (`theme.rs`: Papier, Pro, Neutre; namespace, `extends`; unreadable user ones `skipped`, `notice-themes-skipped`): a deck pins its theme's
  closure (`theme_closure.rs`); `deck_theme.rs` renders with `theme_layer` (pack defaults, then the linearized chain), repairs a merge-broken closure.
  Editing: `theme_edit.rs` (rename), `theme_draft.rs` (draft over its own `extends` list, a child of a built-in under a synthetic root), `theme_extends.rs` (list edits; rename and extends are draft steps, one undo step each), `deck_theme_edit.rs`, `theme_schema.rs` and `theme_composed.rs` (plus packs' tokens), `theme_panel.rs` and `theme_controls.rs`, `theme_picker.rs` (its thumbnails render outside the lock: `theme_job.rs`), `appearance.rs`.
- Widget editing: `widgets_edit.rs`, `widgets_order.rs` (duplicate, reorder), `widget_preview.rs` (working deck's packs), `preview_span.rs` (the preview element's share of the grid, `PreviewSpan`), `widget_examples.rs` (an added element or slide naming its pack's example data gets one copy per data, `data_copy.rs`; previews and pack tabs hold the pack's data; built-ins' `ftl:` markers, data too: `example_text.rs`; a built-in's copy inlines its Fluent labels: `builtin_labels.rs`), `widget_params.rs`, `param_fields.rs`
  (Fluent names, parameter labels and help of built-ins), `items.rs`, `widgets_deck.rs` (`deck`/`deck-layouts` by category); `guides_edit.rs` (the edited pack's or theme's agent guides, one undo step, `Snapshot.guides`; a theme's go through `theme_guides.rs`, a draft step; a draft guide unlike its base's is local); guides reach the agent only if trusted: `guide_origin.rs` (`GuideOrigin`: built-in, any shipped version from the packs' `versions.txt` and `themes/versions.txt`, local, received; a received version with a built-in's shipped guides is active; an approval also covers the received versions of the id with the same guides) and `guide_approvals.rs` (per-version approvals, `guide-approvals.json` in the data folder), asked in the same review (`received_review.rs`; `ApproveGuides`, `LaterReceived`); `panel.rs` (`HostParams::from_schema`, shared by widget and element settings); `data_panel.rs` + `data_edit.rs` (the `host-data` surface of a selected chart or table: the deck data its view names, with its name, views sharing it and the data it can switch to (`bind_choices`), first 99 rows with their ids; a view over a query keeps its settings, its grid the columns it reads of the query's ready result, read-only under its SQL, state and inputs (`query`, `query_page`, `result_plan` over `with_results`, also align's width), its row edits and conversions refused `op-data-derived`; its edits, `bind` as `bind_data`, one undo step each, grid ones as `edit_data` by row and column id; `data_paste.rs`: a paste as `set_cell` plus chained inserts, ids drawn by `prepare_ops`); `element_panel.rs` (the `host-element` surface of a selected element, `element_param` and `element_list` writes, one undo step each); `data_ops.rs` (`prepare_ops` before each apply, `data_undo_label`: `session-undo-data-*`); `request_cap.rs` (`MAX_APPLY_BYTES`, 8 MiB: `check_request_size`, the app's apply commands; `serve` caps its routes' bodies the same); `data_list.rs` (the Data tab's `deck-data` surface: each data with its size and views, a query's state from `query_statuses`; `DataAction` add, add-query, set-query, rename, remove, set-type, cells, add-row, paste, one undo step each, grid ones built by the pane's helpers (`grid_ops`, `add_row_op`, `sheet_paste_ops`) with every view of the data following a column insert or remove from the deck alone, no base read, a view it cannot read refusing it (`session-data-view-unfollowed`); a refusal shown on its row until the next edit; `data-sheet`: one data's rows in the pane, 100 a page, read like the pane's; a query's ready result read-only through the read worker, with its SQL, state, error and inputs, and the last refusal).
- Views: `SlideView.fields` (`fields.rs`), markdown (`text.rs`: headings, lists; `inline.rs`), `search.rs`, `tables.rs`; `Snapshot.host_params`; `Snapshot.warnings`: Typst's, then unknown theme colors (`color_warnings.rs`); add grid (`add_grid.rs`: `add_packs` with seeds, `free_slots`); `carried_assets.rs`: what a paste brings from its source tab (`PasteSource`): assets kept until an op cites them, data copied under fresh ids and names (`data_copy.rs`, `Queries`: a query data as its query in its own deck, in another as manual data over its ready result's base with notice `session-data-query-pasted-as-values`, refused without a result: `QueryPasteNoResult`), refs rewritten, one undo step. Deck knowledge: `knowledge_view.rs` (`knowledge_entries`, `knowledge_file`, `knowledge_search`, both over the first 1 MiB read; a text is open only if its hash is in `knowledge_approvals.rs`, `knowledge-approvals.json`, global and keyed by hash, only growing, written by `approve`: strict read, merge, write; `knowledge_write.rs` approves on write, following ids through a caller batch, refusing an edit or a move of unapproved text and stamping an agent's writes; `approvals_file.rs` reads both approvals files, strictly for that write; `knowledge_review.rs`: the user's review, deck tab only, approving by the hash of the text shown, all or none, and `Snapshot.knowledge`, cached on the bundle generation and approvals count). App-wide box `host-voice`: `voice_view.rs` (text-free `VoiceView`, `SpeechView` with its one localized reason, `voice_event`, `SpeechPatch`), `voice_panel.rs` (`VoicePanel`, data-only refresh; `voice_panel_speech.rs`: the read-aloud section, its reason instead of a switch without a voice, as for barge-in), `voice_fixture.rs`, `pack_fixture.rs` (a pin behind the library) and `knowledge_fixture.rs` (approvals, a seeded knowledge base) under `test-support`.
- Document import: `knowledge_import.rs` (`knowledge_import_job` snapshots an attached document under the lock, `ImportJob::run` extracts it unlocked through the `ExtractRunner`, a dedicated child worker per import, one at a time (a second is refused as busy, never waits), its image and read bounds also capped by the worker memory, `knowledge_import_apply` rechecks the tab and writes `/references/` files through the agent apply path, images staged in `import_staged`, cleared by a drop guard), `knowledge_import_files.rs` (slug, fence-aware parts, frontmatter with the series mark and file hash a re-import checks, banner, image placeholders linked once, `import_overhead`: what an import adds to its text, kept out of the extraction's room).
- Data bases: one `BaseStore` per process (`Platform::bases`); `bases.rs` leases every base the document knows (`sync_bases`, at install and each render; released on drop) and bridges it to the zip (`StoreFiles`): open streams bases in hash-checked, save copies only the current ones; resume keeps the known bases still stored, a paste makes its data's bases known; a merge that replaces a data's layer cuts the undo stacks with a notice (`merge.rs`); `workspace_bases.rs` collects at launch, roots read from every tab folder (none if one is unreadable or the workspace is set aside); `data_read.rs` reads a data's rows from cells or through `data_link.rs` (the data worker, lazy), the read taken under the tab's lock and run with none (`RowsRead`; the data pane's through `workspace_data.rs`, the agent's `data_read` through `data_rows`); a `set_column_type`, a chart-kind change or a show-as on a based data waits the same way for the worker's check or read of its base (`BaseReads`, `BaseWork::run` from `folding`, refused `BaseStale` if the data changed meanwhile). `data_compact.rs` folds a data's overlay (at `COMPACT_AT`, no undo step on it, backoff on failure) into a new base in a silent new layer: snapshot under the tab's lock, worker with none (`workspace_compact.rs`, a thread per job after each request), commit dropped if the data changed; an overlay-cap refusal asks a fold of the data it names (the deck's cap: the largest), only if that makes room (`apply_folding`); `folding` runs it the same way, cuts its undo, then retries once (`Workspace::request`, `TabSession::with_folding`); `replica_gauge.rs` refuses growing ops at 90 % of `max_replica_mib`. Query results: `derived.rs` keys each query (`ResultKey`: engine version, SQL, input keys) and holds its `QueryState` (a ready one carries its table; `query_status`, read by the Data tab, the pane, the agent and MCP: a query data with no status is a bug, logged and failed `session-data-query-no-state`, never pending); after each session acquisition (`acquire`: UI requests, agent, MCP, CLI) `workspace_derived.rs` takes the jobs of changed keys in `query_order`, one per data in flight, runs them in the query worker with no lock, lands them under it (dropped if the key moved; a dropped job clears its in-flight mark) and renders, then tells the host's `Workspace::on_results` sink (Tauri re-emits the view; the dev server UI re-reads after each command); a failed one raises `issue-data-query-failed`, and so do its readers, naming it; a job whose worker was lost or timed out leaves its query pending and runs again, `QUERY_ATTEMPTS` (3) per key, then fails it; `check_query_binds` (`data_panel.rs`) checks a bind against the ready result (`session-data-query-no-result`); a data change redraws its readers' views (`dependent_views`). `query_read.rs`: the agent reads a query data as its ready result (`QueryStatus`; else `QueryReadNoResult`) and previews a SQL text (`QueryPreview`: inputs resolved under the lock, a pending query input refused `QueryInputPending`; run with none, its result's lease dropped, nothing stored). `derived_cache.rs`: open seeds `Ready` from the file's cache when the current key names an entry; save caches only the current ready results; `DeckResults` does both for the CLI, computing the rest blocking.
- Rendering: one `RenderWorker` per `Session`, one `RenderSlot` per render; a tab session starts unrendered, its worker at the first render; float/int casts go through `oku_slides_core::num`. `last_errors.rs`: the last deck render error (`render_deck`, cleared by a success) and failed import (`ImportTab`, on the tab it replaced), in memory, `TabSession::last_errors`.
- `import/`: PowerPoint import, CLI and app (one history step; a retrofit pins `base` and its layout packs, scored by their
  profiles; hi-fi makes a deck-only `deck-layouts` and pins Neutre, its tokens in `deck.tokens`), `render`, `note_overflows`, `reference`, `state` (`ImportState`, tab memory), `score`, `geometry`, tuning (`queue`, `sample`, `diff`, `tune`). Also for the CLI: `library_lookup`, `probed_widgets`.
- `agent/`: what the session offers the agent. `tabs` (`Sessions`, `TabSession`; `document_kind` wakes nothing), turns as undo
  groups (`begin_turn`, reopened after an undo or redo inside, `undo.rs`; `turn_undo` names the deck's atop undo turn, the context's `undoable_turn`), A2UI
  surfaces (`forms`, `forms_confirm` (confirmations, permissions, the heard line), `turn_view`, `receipt` (with its `SpokenText` and the turn's traces), `spoken` (what the read-aloud says: host templates, clipped labels, never agent text in a guard or permission), `render`, `surface_ops`, `usage_panel` over the shared `usage_table` cells), `address`, `variants`, `turn_touches` (elements the agent's `*_by_agent` writes changed, per turn) resolved by `traces` (`Trace`),
  `context` (the turn's block: kind, edited pack or theme, preview theme), `guides` (`AgentGuides`: the framed, sanitized `<oku-slides-guides>` block of the packs and themes a tab uses, sent on an ACP session's first turn and when it does not hold the current digest (`sent_blocks`: session and digest per block, `forget_sent_blocks` after a failed prompt); a received version's text only once approved), `knowledge_index` (the deck tab's `<oku-slides-knowledge>` index of `knowledge_entries`, values bounded by `KnowledgeEntry::bounded` (as `knowledge_list` lists them) and escaped, an awaiting file by path only, capped by `[knowledge] index_entries`/`index_kib` with the rest counted, sent like the guides), `conversation` (one thread, ACP session and work folder uuid per tab, `agent/conversation.json`), `work_dir` (`AgentWorkDir`: the conversation's `work/<uuid>` beside it, minted and found under the lock, prepared or removed outside; `AGENT_CONFIG_NAMES`, the one list of agent config names it strips there and in `work/`, which also loses its non-uuid entries when a uuid is minted), `reason` (`AgentFormReason`); `attachments` (`Session::attach` of an `Upload`, hashed and sniffed once; same bytes keep their first name: pasted images and, in a deck tab, documents sniffed by magic (`media::sniff_document`), in memory per tab, `[paste]` bounds (`AttachLimits`, also `Snapshot.attach_limits` for the draft), `AttachmentRef`; `PASTE_TYPES`, also the only images an agent is shown; `embed_op_assets` also takes attached images newly cited by knowledge files); `app_port` (`AppPort`: update, quit, install; `UnavailableApp` for serve); `workspace_port` (`AgentWorkspace`: the listening tab for the voice router and whether it is focused, `unsaved_tabs`, `Mutex<Workspace>`'s from page reports; the window's tabs, activate, reorder, move, new window, detach, `import_tab`, `open_help` (`workspace_help.rs`: `HELP_HITS` hits searched in the help session), `dirty_revisions` (a close's tabs and their `Session::dirty_revision`, growing per edit, `None` while clean), scoped to the requester's window by `workspace_agent_tabs.rs`, refused as `WorkspaceRefusal`); `context` also has `TurnOrigin` and `AgentUi::within_limits`.

## Workspace

`Workspace` (`workspace.rs`), `WorkspaceRequest` / `WorkspaceView` (`workspace_request.rs`): windows `w-<n>`, tabs `t-<n>` (`_ids`),
detached agent (`agent_window_allow.rs`: what its window may send, `AGENT_WINDOW_LABEL`), `paste` (`PasteElement`: source read, then target applies, outside the lock), `clip` (the copied element for every window: `SetClip`, `Clip`, never saved). `Slot` (`workspace_slot.rs`: `Ready`, `Dormant`, `Closed`) holds a tab's session (`Tab`); other `workspace_*.rs`: `session`
(locking path, `relock`), `locale`, `scheme`, tabs (`Opening`; `ImportTab` builds an imported tab, outcome `Tune` names it for the agent), windows, layout
(`Layout`, `Rect`), resume, same deck, agent, usage (`usage_owner`), help, packs (`OpenPack`: a pack tab after its opener; `PackOpener`, `workspace_opener.rs`: what the opener may take), themes (`OpenTheme`; agents open through the tab gate); `NewPack`/`NewTheme` open an empty draft, which `Trash` deletes if its tab leaves untouched; `opening` (one per id), `import_picks` (each `.pptx` given to `open_files` queues for the focused window in
`WorkspaceView.import_picks`, offered in its import dialog, not a tab, until `ImportPicked` or `DismissImportPicks`; a closed window's picks move; beyond `MAX_PICKS` a notice counts the dropped ones and names three). Lock order: a pack's or theme's opening lock, `Mutex<Workspace>`, then a tab's `Arc<Mutex<Slot>>` (never the reverse; only `try_lock` a session under it), then `DRAFTS`, then `PACK_TABS`.
`quit_barrier.rs` (`QuitBarrier`: the windows a guarded app quit waits for); `own_dir` (`OwnDir`: `new_in`'s random folder under a shared parent, removed on drop). `apply`, `with_session` and `with_quiet` (both share `acquire`), `open_files`, `settle`, `post_notice`, `open_pack_with`, `open_theme_ui`, `pack_opener` take both in sequence, never nested; slow work outside.

`<data>/resume/`: `workspace.json` (layout version 2, counters, `kept`, per-tab `info`: `TabInfo.document`, `tab_document.rs`, with legacy keys) and
`tabs/t-<n>/{state.json, deck/, pack/, agent/conversation.json, work/<uuid>/}`, each written by its session only; `deck/` holds `deck.loro`, `document.json` (`usage_unsaved` too), `usage.jsonl` (written on change), `assets/`, `fonts/`, `packs/`. Unreadable parts are set
aside as `*.failed-…` (state version 3, 5 in pack mode and theme tabs, a theme tab keeping only `state.json`; an older unsaved tab: `notice-tab-set-aside`). Tabs restore lazily from `info` (ADR-0001, ADR-0011, ADR-0012).
